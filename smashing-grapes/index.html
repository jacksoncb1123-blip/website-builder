The Philosophy

A table set where
old world meets new.

Smashing Grapes is an upscale kitchen and wine bar built on a simple idea — that great food and great wine belong together, in a room worth lingering in. Our menu travels the globe with a Mediterranean heart: handmade pastas, artisan pizzas, fresh seafood, and premium cuts.

The cellar spans Spain, Argentina, Germany, California, and South Africa, with local Maryland & Virginia pours celebrating our own terroir. Chic yet comfortable — like dining in a vineyard.
