Our story

Good food starts
with good neighbors.

We opened Saffron & Sage with a simple belief: the best meals come from the best ingredients, grown by people we know. Every morning we build our menu around what arrives from nearby farms — so no two visits are ever quite the same.

Our kitchen keeps things honest and seasonal. Our dining room keeps things warm. Whether it's a quiet dinner for two or a long table of friends, you're family while you're here.

  • Local sourcing from farms within 50 miles
  • Seasonal menu that changes with the harvest
  • Natural wines & house-made cocktails
